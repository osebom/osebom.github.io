OJ Onyeagwu

— in Toronto

I'm a data engineer at CIBC, where I build pipelines and platforms that move financial data at scale. Before that I was a backend intern at Vector Institute, working on applied ML for user experience research.

On the side, I'm a bit obsessed with agents — designing small systems that can plan, retrieve, and reason on their own. I work mostly in Python and TypeScript, with FastAPI, LangChain, Cohere, and a stack of vector stores I keep swapping in and out.

I care about data, minimalism, and sports — usually in that order. Most of my projects start as a question I can't stop asking, and end up as something small and useful I can show a friend.

Off the clock, I'm watching films, listening to music, and counting down to the 2026 World Cup.
